# Code of conduct

story-forge follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

- Be respectful and constructive with everyone, in English, Spanish or any other language.
- Critique ideas, not people. Assume good intent; ask before judging.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private information.
- Never share confidential data of your company, your clients or anyone else in issues, pull requests or examples.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and block participants who do not follow this code. Report problems privately through GitHub's reporting tools or by contacting the maintainer listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`.
