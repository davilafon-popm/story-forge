# Design summary — Schedule transfer (fictional)

Screen S1 «Schedule transfer» — texts: "From account", "To account", "Amount", "Frequency", "One time" / "Recurring", button "Continue" (disabled until fields are complete).
Screen S2 «Recurrence» — "Frequency" (Weekly, Every two weeks, Monthly), "Start date", "End date (optional)", validation message "The end date must be after the start date."
Screen S3 «Confirm» — summary and button "Schedule".
Screen S4 «Done!» — "Your transfer is scheduled." button "Go to home".
Screen S5 «Error» — "We couldn't schedule your transfer." "Please try again." button "Retry".
Designer note (Dev Note): "Max 12 active schedules per customer."
